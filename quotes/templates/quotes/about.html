Sun Tzu

About Sun Tzu

Sun Tzu was a Chinese military strategist, philosopher, and the reputed author of The Art of War, an influential treatise on strategy and warfare. Believed to have lived during the Eastern Zhou period (circa 5th century BCE), his teachings emphasize intelligence, adaptability, and psychological tactics over brute force. The Art of War remains a cornerstone in military doctrine, business strategy, and competitive thinking worldwide.


Why Sun Tzu Is Important

This web application was created to bring the timeless wisdom of Sun Tzu to a modern audience. It aims to make his insights easily accessible to all. Whether you're a strategist, a history enthusiast, or just someone who enjoys thought-provoking quotes, I hope this project inspires you to think critically and strategically.
